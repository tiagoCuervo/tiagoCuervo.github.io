## A compact argument

Begin with the question the note addresses, explain why it matters, and state
the central claim early. The surrounding layout is designed for long-form
technical writing without making a short note feel sparse.

<Callout title="Main idea">
  Callouts isolate the one statement a reader should carry into the next section.
</Callout>

## Evidence and interpretation

Use ordinary Markdown for prose, lists, links, equations, and code. Figures can
be added with the reusable `Figure` component shown in `_template.mdx`.

<Sidenote>
  Sidenotes hold useful context that would otherwise interrupt the argument.
</Sidenote>

Set `draft: false` in the frontmatter when the article is ready. It will then
appear automatically on the homepage, writing index, RSS feed, sitemap, and its
own public route.
